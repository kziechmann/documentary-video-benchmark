# Brief — Twenty-one pictures: Mariner 4 at Mars, 1965

**Task:** a2-0001-mariner4 · **Variant:** A2 (write and cut) · **Runtime:** 120–180 seconds

## Commission

Produce a short archival-style documentary segment about NASA's Mariner 4 mission, the first spacecraft to return close-up pictures of another planet. Write the narration yourself from the source pack, render it with the provided narrator voice (`A01`), and cut it from the asset bundle.

## Audience and tone

General adult audience with no space background. Measured, curious, unhurried. Think public-broadcast history strand, not hype reel. No rhetorical questions in narration. Plain language; explain any technical term the first time it appears.

## Required beats (in this order)

1. **Hook** — the first picture of Mars was finished by hand, with colored pencils, before the computer had drawn it.
2. **Context** — the 1964 launch campaign, including the sister spacecraft that failed three weeks earlier.
3. **The flyby** — the July 1965 encounter and how slowly the pictures came home.
4. **What the pictures showed** — and what they ruled out.
5. **Legacy** — one or two sentences on why this mattered for everything that followed.

## Must-include facts

Narration must state each of these (figures as given in the source pack):

- The launch date and launch vehicle of Mariner 4
- The fate of Mariner 3
- The date of the Mars flyby
- The number of complete pictures returned
- The data rate, and roughly how long one picture took to transmit
- What the pictures revealed about the Martian surface

## Must-exclude

- No imagery from any later Mars mission presented as Mariner 4 imagery. Later imagery may be used only as an **illustration**, with an on-screen label reading `ILLUSTRATION — [mission, year]` for its full duration.
- No claims about the presence or absence of life on Mars unless the source pack directly supports them.
- No music under the archival newsreel narration in `V01` when that narration is audible; use it as a sound bite or not at all.

## Graphics

- Lower-third on first appearance of any named person, spacecraft, or facility: name on line 1, descriptor on line 2.
- Every archival clip or still carries a small corner date tag (year) for its first 3 seconds.
- Title card at open; source credit card at close (list every asset used by origin).

## Disclosure

Any shot that is not period footage of the subject it accompanies (modern imagery, animation, re-creation) must be flagged `synthetic` or `illustrative` in `manifest.json` and labeled on screen as above.

## Citations

Every factual claim in `script.json` must cite at least one source-pack document by `doc_id` and, where possible, a line or section anchor. Prefer primary and curated sources; the pack contains material of mixed reliability.
